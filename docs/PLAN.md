# Gargancoin: Build Plan

## The premise

Garganco is an LLC that, as far as the open internet is concerned, does not
exist. No website, no directory listing, no press. That absence is the whole
opportunity: we are not parodying a company with a known shape, we are
*inventing the shape* and backdating it.

The funniest version of this bit is **not** a coin. It is a fully-realized
fake industrial conglomerate that happens to issue scrip. Pittsburgh, steel,
company towns, company stores, mid-century annual reports. Gargancoin is the
company scrip. That frame is funnier, more buildable, and carries near-zero
legal risk compared to "we made a crypto."

## Brand spine

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Garganco — "Diversified Holdings" |
| Token | Gargancoin, ticker **$GARG** |
| Subunit | 100 garglets = 1 GARG |
| Palette | Ink black, brass, bone paper, slate |
| Type | Archivo Narrow (wordmark), Spectral (prospectus body) |
| Register | Deadpan. Never wink. The comedy is total sincerity. |
| Tagline | "Redeemable in full at the company store." |

**Load-bearing joke:** every artifact is over-produced relative to the thing
it describes. A 12-page typeset litepaper about nothing. An annual report
with audited figures for a firm with one filing cabinet.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — The Bit (a weekend, $0–15)

1. **Domain.** `gargancoin.com`, `garganco.com`, `gargancoin.xyz`, and
   `garganco.co` all had no DNS records as of this writing — likely available.
   `garganco.com` is the prize; grab it before the bit outgrows it.
2. **Landing page.** Build out from the holding page in this repo: hero,
   fake ticker, tokenomics, roadmap, divisions, investor relations.
3. **The Litepaper.** The centerpiece. ~10 pages, properly typeset, PDF.
   Abstract, monetary theory, consensus mechanism ("Proof of Matthew"),
   governance, risk factors. Zero jokes in the formatting; all jokes in
   the content.
4. **Marks.** Wordmark, coin SVG, square PFP crop, favicon.
5. **Lore bible.** Founding myth, division list, corporate timeline. Every
   later artifact pulls from this so the canon stays consistent.

### Phase 2 — Make it feel alive (a few evenings)

6. **Price oracle.** Random walk plus event-driven jumps tied to real-world
   Matthew activity. "Matthew replied to the group text — GARG +14%."
7. **Investor relations.** Quarterly shareholder letters, actually emailed.
   This is the engine that keeps the bit running for years on low effort.
8. **Annual report.** One per year. Charts. A letter from the Chairman.
9. **Merch.** Embroidered work shirt with a Garganco patch, enamel pin,
   mug. Print-on-demand, no inventory. The work shirt is the single best
   physical object in this whole plan.
10. **Certificate of ownership.** Engraved-style share certificate, printed
    and framed, given to Matthew. This is the gift that lands the joke in
    person — do this one even if you do nothing else.

### Phase 3 — On-chain, no market ($1–50)

11. Deploy $GARG as an SPL token on Solana (cheapest) or an ERC-20 on Base.
12. Airdrop by hand to family wallets. Fixed supply. Metadata points at
    the site.
13. **Never create a liquidity pool.** No pair, no price, no listing. It is
    genuinely worthless and says so on the tin.

The appeal of this tier: a real, permanent, public on-chain artifact with
the family name on it, at roughly the cost of a sandwich — with none of the
exposure that comes from anyone being able to buy it.

### Phase 4 — The cliff

Adding liquidity, launching on pump.fun, or doing anything that lets a
stranger buy $GARG changes the category entirely. At that point it is a
financial product: securities and commodities regulators, money-transmission
rules, and ordinary fraud liability all attach, and "it was a joke" has not
worked as a defense for anyone who has tried it. Real enforcement actions and
private suits have followed celebrity and novelty coin launches.

Recommendation: stop at Phase 3. The bit is not funnier with a market
attached, and it becomes materially less funny the moment someone loses
money on it.

## Gating items before anything goes public

- **Matthew has to be in on it.** It is his name and his LLC. Everything in
  Phases 1–3 is affectionate, but it should be a surprise he is delighted by,
  not one he finds out about from a search result.
- **Keep it visibly parody.** The site must never imply the token is issued
  by or affiliated with the actual operating LLC. The disclaimer on the
  holding page already does this — keep it on every public surface.
- **Don't touch the real business.** No use of actual Garganco branding,
  clients, filings, or finances. The invented conglomerate and the real LLC
  should share a name and nothing else.

## Roadmap (in-universe)

- **Q1** — Acquire filing cabinet.
- **Q2** — Acquire second filing cabinet.
- **Q3** — Reconcile the two filing cabinets.
- **Q4** — Annual shareholders meeting. A Pittsburgh bar. A slide deck. A
  catered tray. This is the actual finale of the bit and everything above
  is production design for it.
